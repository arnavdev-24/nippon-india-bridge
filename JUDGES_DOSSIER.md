# Page 1: Executive Summary & The Macro Problem

### The Macro Problem: Why Japanese Market Entry in India Stalls
India is one of the highest-growth manufacturing and agricultural mechanization markets in the world, while Japan possesses world-class precision engineering, electric powertrain technology, and quality control systems. However, **over 80% of Japanese mid-market enterprises (Chuken/SME) struggle to scale in India** due to three compounding failure modes:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      THE BILATERAL EXPANSION CHASM                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
       🇯🇵 Japanese Tech / OEM                     🇮🇳 Indian Target Market
  ┌───────────────────────────────┐          ┌───────────────────────────────┐
  │ High-Precision Machinery      │          │ 28 Heterogeneous States       │
  │ Strict Tolerance Standards    │   ───►   │ Sticky Black Vertisol Soils   │
  │ Ringi-sho Consensus Protocol  │   ───►   │ High-Dust Post-Harvest Stubble│
  │ Formal Contract Expectation   │   ───►   │ Informal Dealer Trust Networks│
  │ Single-Nation Homogeneity     │          │ Complex DBT & SMAM Subsidies  │
  └───────────────────────────────┘          └───────────────────────────────┘
                                       ▲
                                       │ FAILURE MODES:
                 ┌─────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
                 │ 1. Engineering Mismatch (Slurry / Heat)   │
                 │ 2. Regulatory Lock-out (No FMTTI/MahaDBT)  │
                 │ 3. Asymmetric Information & Distrust       │
                 └────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Engineering Misalignment:** Japanese equipment engineered for Japanese volcanic/alluvial soil frequently suffers severe axle seal failure in flooded wet-paddy puddling (Punjab/Bengal) or rotor tine clogging in sticky Vertisols (Madhya Pradesh/Maharashtra).
2. **State-Level Regulatory Fragmentation:** Agricultural machinery cannot sell at scale without government Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) subsidies. Every state requires separate portal registration (SMAM Budni FMTTI, MahaDBT, MP e-Krishi Anudan, Karnataka Raitha Siri, Odisha Krushak Portal).
3. **Information Asymmetry & Cultural Decoupling:** Japanese executives make decisions through structured corporate consensus (*Ringi-sho* and *Nemawashi*), while Indian regional distributors rely on high-velocity relational negotiations and WhatsApp spare parts ordering.

# Page 2: The 100-Point Matching Algorithm & State Intelligence

Unlike generic business directories that rely on keyword searches, our platform utilizes an **objective, multi-factor compatibility scoring engine** specifically weighted for cross-border industrial joint ventures.

### Compatibility Score Breakdown (Total: 100 Points)

| Category | Weight | Evaluation Criteria & Formula Factors |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Capital & Financial Scale** | **25 Pts** | Evaluates equity capacity, working capital headroom, pilot batch commitment (10–30 units), and margin expectations (12%–18% standard). |
| **Regulatory & Subsidy Readiness** | **25 Pts** | Assesses active portal empanelment (Central SMAM/FMTTI Budni report, state DBT portals like MahaDBT, e-Krishi Anudan, Rythu Bharosa). |
| **Engineering & Soil Adaptation** | **25 Pts** | Checks component capability against regional field stress: ground clearance (≥280mm), dual-lip slurry seals, cyclonic air pre-cleaners, high-tensile 10.9 bolts. |
| **Field SLA & Service Infrastructure** | **25 Pts** | Verifies dealer buffer stock hubs, mobile technician guarantee (within 24–72 hours), regional language manuals, and warranty turnaround speed. |

```
                       TOTAL SCORE = C_cap + R_reg + E_eng + S_sla
       ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
       │ Capital (25 pts)│ Regs (25 pts)   │ Eng (25 pts)    │ Service (25 pts)│
       └─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
                                            │
                                            ▼
                    Final Match Tier Classification:
                    • 90 – 100 : Preferred Strategic Partner (Direct Term Sheet)
                    • 75 – 89  : High Compatibility (Minor Soil/Service Adjustment)
                    • 60 – 74  : Conditional Fit (Requires Pilot Testing & Subsidy Filing)
                    • < 60     : Disqualified / Incompatible
```

### ⛔ Hard Deal-Breakers (Instant Disqualification)
If any of the following deal-breakers occur, the match is scored as **0 / Disqualified** regardless of other points:
1. **Zero DBT / FMTTI Testing Compliance:** The partner has neither testing certificates nor an active pathway to obtain Budni FMTTI approval, legally barring them from farmer subsidy sales.
2. **Incompatible Working Clearance:** Equipment width exceeds row-crop trellises (e.g. >550 mm in grape vineyards) or ground clearance is under 200 mm for flooded wetland puddling.
3. **No 72-Hour Service SLA:** Partner refuses to maintain buffer spare parts within a 150 km cluster radius, violating standard agricultural warranty continuity.

### Tri-Persona Architecture & Live Walkthrough Path

The platform includes a persistent **Persona Switcher** allowing judges and users to evaluate the platform from three perspectives with a single click:

```
┌─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
│ 🇯🇵 Japanese Manufacturer│   🇮🇳 Indian JV Partner  │🚜 Field Dealer/Stockist │
├─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
│ • State Feasibility Map │ • Post Technology Pitch │ • Soil & Field Feedback │
│ • Launch Pilot Campaign │ • Capability Audit Form │ • Request Spare Parts   │
│ • Browse Indian JVs     │ • Review Bilateral Deals│ • Subsidy Portal Status │
└─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┘
```

#### 5-Minute Evaluation Route for Judges:
1. **Start as Japanese Manufacturer:**
   - Navigate to `/market-intelligence` (State Intelligence). Select **Maharashtra** or **Gujarat** to see power rates, industrial corridors, and incentive matrices.
   - Go to `/campaigns` to inspect the **Compact Rotary Tiller Pilot** campaign and see real-time dealer feedback analytics.
2. **Switch to Indian JV Partner:**
   - Navigate to `/joint-ventures` to view 8 pre-seeded bilateral enterprise profiles (e.g. *Sahyadri Precision Agro*, *Kyoto Robotics*, *Belagavi Agri Solutions*).
   - Click **Connect** to test the collaborative partnership request modal.
3. **Switch to Field Dealer / Distributor:**
   - Navigate to `/surveys` to review the active **Field Adaptation Questionnaire**.
   - Review `/distributors` to explore verified machinery stockists with regional service SLA ratings.
