⚠️ The SME Challenge in India
  • Monolith Fallacy: India is 28 diverse economies with differing soils, monsoons, and local subsidies.
  • High Advisory Costs: Big-4 consulting reports cost \$50K+ without giving practical dealer access.
  • Product Mismatch: Japanese specs often fail local field conditions (e.g. soil clearance, 45°C heat).
  • Regulatory Hurdles: Navigating BIS certification, GST, and CEPA concessions without local partners.
✅ The Nippon2Bharat Solution
  • Ground Fieldwork Engine: Commission verified dealer surveys with live analytics & PDF export.
  • State Intelligence: Pinpoint Japanese Industrial Townships (JITs) & state DBT subsidies.
  • Maruti-Style JV Matchmaker: Connect with vetted Indian manufacturing & corporate partners.
  • Direct Advisory Access: Book vetted CAs, CSs, and bilingual corporate Japanese/Hindi tutors.

Five Bilateral Platform Pillars

Complete End-to-End Corridor
01 / SURVEYS

Ground Fieldwork Engine

Launch dynamic dealer campaigns. Includes mobile QR surveys and a smart Excel/TSV ingestion engine with fuzzy choice matching and auto-assigned provisional GSTINs.

02 / STATE INTEL

17-State Intelligence

Regional cluster analytics mapping Japanese Industrial Townships (JITs), state-level capital subsidies (MahaDBT, Ikhedut, SMAM), and port logistics connectivity.

03 / JOINT VENTURE

JV & Technology Exchange

Bilateral matching based on complementary capabilities (e.g. Japanese precision CNC IP × Indian Tier-1 OEM manufacturing floor space & supply contracts).

04 / DISTRIBUTORS

28-State Distributor Hub

Directory of vetted regional distributors filterable by cold-chain logistics, bonded warehousing, CDSCO/BIS import licenses, and after-sales service teams.

05 / REGULATORY

CEPA & Tax Advisory

Direct scheduling with ICAI-certified CAs and Company Secretaries. Built-in India–Japan CEPA tariff reduction simulator for duty-free machinery imports.

06 / TALENT

Corporate Language Hub

Bilingual JLPT & Business Hindi training for cross-border expansion teams with live calendar slot scheduling for certified native instructors.
